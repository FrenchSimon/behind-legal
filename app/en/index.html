Behind Français

Behind-the-scenes stories at the right moment while you watch.

Behind tells you the behind-the-scenes story of the movie you’re watching, right as you watch it. No spoilers, and never during a big scene.

Get it on Google Play Android, phone and tablet · no account, no ads

How it works

Start the movie, Behind does the rest.

1

Start with the movie

Press Play at the same time as the movie. Starting late? Say where you are and Behind catches up.

2

A buzz, a story

At the right moment, your phone gives a gentle buzz: a production story appears in three lines. Expand the card to learn more.

3

The recap and the quiz

After the credits, find every story in its long version, then test what you remember.

Made for the dark

Designed to spoil nothing.

  • Never during a big scene: the story waits for the quiet moment that follows.
  • No spoilers: only what you’ve already seen.
  • About one story every ten minutes, no more.
  • A very dark screen, and alerts even with the screen locked.
  • Out of sync? Pause, nudge buttons and key scenes help you find your place.
