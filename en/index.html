Privacy policy

Updated on October 27, 2026

In short

Behind doesn’t ask you for an account or any personal data. What the app keeps stays on your device. The only thing it sends is anonymous statistics, and only if you agree.

Who is responsible

Behind is published by Simon Dabin. For any question about your data, write to behind.app.contact@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

To work, Behind keeps on your phone or tablet: the session in progress, to pick the movie up where you left off (for 12 hours at most); the movies you have watched, for the recap; the setup you already did; your keep-screen-on preference; your choice about statistics; and the alerts scheduled during the movie.

This data never leaves your device. “Delete my data”, in Settings, deletes all of it and cancels the alerts. Uninstalling the app deletes it too.

Anonymous statistics

They are optional: nothing is sent until you have agreed. They are only used to find out whether Behind is useful during a movie.

If you agree, Behind sends three pieces of information: a session started (which movie, from the beginning or partway through), an anecdote opened (which one) and a movie finished. Technical information is added: system, app version, language, country and region, inferred from your IP address, which is not kept.

No identifier comes with them: they cannot be linked to you. They are received by Aptabase, an analytics service hosted in the European Union (in Germany), which keeps them for 5 years at most.

They rely on your consent. You can withdraw it at any time with the “Anonymous statistics” switch in Settings: nothing is sent after that.

What Behind doesn’t do

No account, no ads, no tracking across apps, no advertising identifier, no sale of data. Behind doesn’t ask for your location and receives no remote notifications: alerts are scheduled on your device.

Your rights

You can withdraw your consent to statistics and delete your data at any time, from Settings. Notification permission can be withdrawn in your phone’s settings. Since statistics already sent are anonymous, no one can find them to delete them.

For any question or request, write to behind.app.contact@gmail.com. You can also file a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority (cnil.fr).

Updates

This policy was last updated on October 27, 2026. If it changes in an important way, Behind will let you know in the app.
